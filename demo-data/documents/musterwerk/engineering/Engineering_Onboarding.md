# Engineering Onboarding Guide

Synthetic demonstration document for the fictional Musterwerk GmbH.

## 1 Before the First Day

HR sends the employment contract and the IT account request. The team lead assigns an onboarding buddy from the same team.

## 2 First Week

- Day 1: collect laptop and badge at the IT service desk, safety introduction with the safety officer.
- Day 2: access to the Git server, the PLM system, and the ticket system.
- Day 3: workshop tour and introduction to the MW-400 press.
- Day 4 and 5: shadow your buddy on current tickets.

## 3 Mandatory Trainings

Within the first 30 days every engineer completes: machine safety (4 hours), electrical safety for skilled persons (if applicable), information security awareness (1 hour), and export control basics (1 hour).

## 4 Probation Goals

During the six-month probation period, new engineers agree on three goals with their team lead, including one independently completed customer ticket.
