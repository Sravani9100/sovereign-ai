# Procurement Policy 2025

Synthetic demonstration document for the fictional Musterwerk GmbH. Valid from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025.

## 1 Purchase Requests

Every purchase above 500 euros requires a purchase request in the ERP system.

## 2 Quotes

For purchases above 5,000 euros, at least three written quotes must be obtained. The cheapest suitable offer must be chosen, or the choice must be justified in writing.

## 3 Approval Limits

- Up to 5,000 euros: team lead
- Up to 25,000 euros: department head
- Above 25,000 euros: managing director

## 4 Suppliers

New suppliers are approved by the purchasing department after a credit check.
