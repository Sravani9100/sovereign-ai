# Procurement Policy 2026

Synthetic demonstration document for the fictional Musterwerk GmbH. Valid from 1 January 2026. Replaces the Procurement Policy 2025.

## 1 Purchase Requests

Every purchase above 1,000 euros requires a purchase request in the supplier portal. The ERP purchase request form is no longer used.

## 2 Quotes

For purchases above 10,000 euros, at least three written quotes must be obtained through the supplier portal.

## 3 Approval Limits

- Up to 10,000 euros: team lead
- Up to 50,000 euros: department head
- Above 50,000 euros: managing director and CFO

## 4 Suppliers

New suppliers must complete the supplier code of conduct and a sustainability self-assessment (LkSG) before their first order.
