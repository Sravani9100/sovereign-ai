# Customer Complaint Escalation Procedure

Synthetic demonstration document for the fictional Musterwerk GmbH.

## 1 Scope

This procedure applies to complaints from customers about delivered machines, spare parts, or service visits.

## 2 Severity Levels

- Level 1: a question or minor issue with no production impact. Response within 2 working days.
- Level 2: degraded production at the customer. Response within 8 hours.
- Level 3: a customer production line is stopped, or a safety issue. Response within 2 hours, 24/7.

## 3 Escalation Steps

### 3.1 First Response

The service hotline records every complaint as a ticket in the service system and assigns a severity level.

### 3.2 Escalation to Engineering

Level 2 and Level 3 tickets are escalated to the on-call service engineer. If the issue is not resolved within 24 hours, the head of service is informed.

### 3.3 Management Escalation

Level 3 tickets that are not resolved within 48 hours are escalated to the managing director. The key account manager informs the customer about the escalation.

## 4 Closing a Complaint

A complaint is closed only after the customer confirms the solution. Root causes are reviewed in the monthly quality meeting.
