# Musterwerk GmbH Employee Handbook 2026

Synthetic demonstration document. Musterwerk GmbH is a fictional company.

## 1 Welcome

Musterwerk GmbH builds hydraulic presses and industrial automation systems in Stuttgart. This handbook applies to all employees with a German employment contract from 1 January 2026.

## 2 Working Hours

### 2.1 Regular Hours

The regular working time is 38 hours per week for full-time employees. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Thursday, and 10:00 to 13:00 on Friday.

### 2.2 Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Approved overtime is credited to your working-time account and can be taken as time off within six months.

## 3 Remote Work

Remote work rules are described in the separate Remote Work Policy 2026.

## 4 Leave

### 4.1 Public Holidays

Musterwerk follows the public holidays of Baden-Württemberg. 24 December and 31 December are additional company holidays.

### 4.2 Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 30 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working days per week. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance and approved by your manager. Up to five unused days can be carried over to 31 March of the following year.

### 4.3 Sick Leave

If you are unable to work because of illness, inform your manager before 09:00 on the first day. From the third calendar day of illness a medical certificate (AU-Bescheinigung) is required.

### 4.4 Parental Leave

Parental leave follows the German Federal Parental Allowance and Parental Leave Act (BEEG). Inform HR at least seven weeks before the planned start.

## 5 Equipment and IT

Every employee receives a company laptop. Personal devices must not be used to store company data. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

## 6 Code of Conduct

### 6.1 Gifts

Employees may not accept gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than 35 euros. Gifts above this value must be reported to Compliance.

### 6.2 Confidentiality

Customer drawings, prices, and personnel information are confidential and must not be shared outside the company.
