# Travel Reimbursement Rules

Synthetic demonstration document for the fictional Musterwerk GmbH. Valid from 1 March 2026.

## 1 Approval

Business trips must be approved by your manager in the travel tool before booking. Trips outside the EU additionally require approval by the department head.

## 2 Transport

### 2.1 Rail

Train travel is the default for trips under 500 km. Second class is reimbursed; first class only for journeys longer than four hours.

### 2.2 Flights

Flights are allowed for trips over 500 km or when the train journey takes more than six hours. Economy class only. Flights must be booked through the company travel agency.

### 2.3 Private Car

Use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre, only if no train connection is reasonable.

## 3 Accommodation

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 120 euros per night in Germany and up to 160 euros per night in Munich, Hamburg, and Frankfurt, including breakfast.

## 4 Meals (Per Diem)

The daily allowance follows the German tax rates: 28 euros for a full day away and 14 euros for arrival and departure days or absences of more than eight hours. If a meal is provided, the allowance is reduced (breakfast 20%, lunch and dinner 40% each).

## 5 Submitting Expenses

Submit expenses in the travel tool within 30 days after the trip, with all receipts attached as photos or PDFs. Reimbursement is paid with the next salary.
