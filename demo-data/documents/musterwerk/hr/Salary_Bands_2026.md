# Salary Bands 2026 (Confidential)

Synthetic demonstration document for the fictional Musterwerk GmbH. Access is restricted to HR.

## 1 Engineering

- Junior Engineer (E1): 52,000 to 60,000 euros per year
- Engineer (E2): 60,000 to 72,000 euros per year
- Senior Engineer (E3): 72,000 to 88,000 euros per year
- Principal Engineer (E4): 88,000 to 105,000 euros per year

## 2 Service

- Service Technician (S1): 45,000 to 54,000 euros per year
- Senior Service Technician (S2): 54,000 to 64,000 euros per year

## 3 Rules

Salary reviews take place every April. Increases above the band maximum require approval by the managing director. Individual salaries are never shared outside HR and the employee's manager.
