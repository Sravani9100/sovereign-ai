# RT-Pump Series Service Manual

Synthetic demonstration document for the fictional RheinTech GmbH. Confidential: RheinTech internal.

## Error Codes

### E104 Coolant Level Low

The coolant reservoir is below the minimum mark. Top up with RheinTech coolant RT-C7 only, then reset the controller.

### E220 Motor Overcurrent

The pump motor drew more than 18 A. Check the impeller for blockage.
