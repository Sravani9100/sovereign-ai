# RheinTech GmbH Employee Handbook

Synthetic demonstration document. RheinTech GmbH is a fictional company in Cologne.

## 1 Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week after the probation period. Remote work abroad is not permitted.

## 2 Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 28 working days of paid annual leave. Unused leave expires on 31 December.

## 3 Equipment

RheinTech provides a laptop and a one-time home office allowance of 150 euros.
